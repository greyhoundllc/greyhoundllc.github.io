Real estate development & investment · Northern California

Investing in the future of the built environment.

Greyhound LLC is a real estate development and investment company focused on mixed use properties, redevelopment and adaptive reuse in Northern California. We are at the intersection of real estate economics, architecture and long term asset strategy.

01

What we do

We develop and evaluate mixed-use real estate with an emphasis on long-term value creation and bringing new life to small-town America.

We seek out overlooked potential, like an upper floor that could become housing or coworking space, an underused storefront that could support a new business or new evening uses that keep a downtown active beyond the workday. Sometimes creating value means developing something new. Other times it means finding a creative lease structure that helps an existing business stay and grow.

We believe the strongest downtowns are diverse, active and distinctly their own. Whenever possible, we preserve and adapt buildings with architectural, artistic or historic significance, while finding new uses that make them economically relevant for the future.

For us, development is about more than buildings. It is about creating places where people live, work, gather and participate in the life of a community.

02

Our approach

We approach real estate as both an investment discipline and a systems problem.

Every property exists within a larger set of interconnected variables, including capital requirements, construction costs, operating economics, market demand, building performance, regulation, infrastructure and community context.

Rather than evaluating these factors independently, we look at them holistically and study how different development decisions affect the performance of an asset over time.

We consider the physical, financial, regulatory and market conditions surrounding each asset to identify strategies that can improve both near-term performance and long term value.

03

Why small town America

Small towns offer a distinctive environment for exploring the future of real estate. We seek out small towns that combine significant historic architecture with an established urban core.

Small town America faces many challenges such as an aging building stock, changing commercial demand, housing needs, rising construction costs, seismic requirements and the difficulty of financing substantial improvements in markets where development costs and achievable rents may not align. These conditions create complex development questions, but also significant opportunities for thoughtful reinvestment.

Greyhound LLC is interested in how existing properties can evolve to support a more economically resilient and active urban environment while retaining the architectural character that makes small town America distinctive.

04

Mixed use development

Mixed use properties have the ability to evolve alongside the communities they serve.

Combining residential, commercial, professional, hospitality, and other uses can create more resilient assets while contributing to active and walkable urban environments.

Greyhound LLC evaluates mixed use development through both an investment and urban perspective, considering how the combination of uses affects property performance, tenant demand, neighborhood activity, and long term adaptability.

We are particularly interested in development strategies that allow properties to respond to changing economic and community needs without losing the qualities that make them distinctive.

05

Adaptive reuse & historic Main Street revitalization

Existing buildings represent both an asset and a challenge.

They contain embedded materials, architecture, history, and economic value, while often carrying significant constraints related to age, configuration, seismic performance, accessibility, energy efficiency, maintenance and changing patterns of use.

Greyhound LLC explores how redevelopment and adaptive reuse can extend the productive life of these properties. Our focus is on identifying interventions that improve performance and create new uses while recognizing the economic realities of redevelopment.

We are particularly interested in the question of how high quality reinvestment can remain financially viable in smaller markets.

06

Technology and research

Greyhound LLC is exploring how emerging technologies can improve the way real estate investment and development decisions are made.

Advances in data analytics and artificial intelligence create new opportunities to integrate this information and evaluate development scenarios more dynamically.

Our current interests include the use of technology to support feasibility analysis, redevelopment scenarios, capital allocation, asset management, and the evaluation of economic, environmental and social outcomes.

Technology is not the objective. Better information, stronger analysis, and better built environment decisions are.
